BID ASSISTANT FOR WHATNOT: USER GUIDE
=====================================

WHAT IT DOES
A side panel for Chrome that follows the live auction on a Whatnot show: item,
current bid, exact time left, and whether it is Sudden Death. It can bid for
you with one tap, or automatically. It uses the Whatnot tab you are already
logged into and never asks for your password.

INSTALL
1. Unzip the extension folder.
2. In Chrome, go to chrome://extensions and turn on "Developer mode" (top right).
3. Click "Load unpacked" and select the unzipped folder.
4. Pin the extension (puzzle icon > pin) for easy access.
5. Open (or reload) a Whatnot live show, then click the extension icon.
   The panel opens on the right.

BID BUTTON
Works like Whatnot's own bid button. One tap places a real bid at the amount
shown (e.g. "Bid £24"). No max needed. It won't bid if you're already winning.

AUTO-BID
1. Set "Max bid". It will never bid above this.
2. Choose a mode:
   - Snipe: one bid just before the end ("Bid at ... ms left").
     On normal auctions it re-snipes if you're outbid and the timer resets.
   - Snipe + re-bid: as Snipe. If someone outbids you after your snipe and
     there is still enough time ("Re-bid if >= ... ms left"), it bids again
     straight away. Best for Sudden Death.
   - Keep winning: bids whenever you're not the top bidder, at any time,
     up to your max.
3. Tap "Arm auto-bid" on the current auction. It disarms when the auction ends.

DRY RUN VS LIVE
Auto-bid starts in Dry run: it shows what it would bid but sends nothing.
Flip the switch to Live and confirm to place real bids. Live switches back to
Dry run whenever the page reloads or you change show.

TIPS
- Keep the Whatnot tab visible. Chrome slows down background tabs.
- Start with "Bid at 1000 ms" and lower it gradually. Bids take roughly
  50-60 ms to reach Whatnot.
- "Closing..." means the timer has hit zero and Whatnot hasn't confirmed the
  end yet (usually about 1 second).
- If the panel says "Reload the Whatnot tab", do that. It happens after
  installing or updating the extension.

IMPORTANT
Every accepted bid is binding, just like bidding by hand. Automated bidding may
be against Whatnot's terms, so use it at your own risk.
